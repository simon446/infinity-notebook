# Infinity Notebook

This notebook contains every piece of text that will ever exist (limited to 2000 Unicode characters).

Try to edit this text and press enter to search for that page in the notebook.

To change the page number, click on the page number on top of the page and change it to whatever you like.

To write multiline text, press Shift + Enter.

The pages are sorted by a small language model (SmolLM2-135M) that runs in your browser: the less a text surprises it, the lower its page number. Your first search downloads the model (about 140 MB), so be patient.
