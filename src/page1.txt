# Infinity Notebook

Every text that fits on one page already has a page in this notebook. A page holds whatever fits in a link of 2000 characters: several thousand characters of ordinary writing, less of gibberish.

Press the pencil in the corner and write something, then press **Find page** to open the page it is on. Until then your writing is kept as a draft in this browser.

The pencil also brings out the page number, arrows to flip pages, a dice for a random page, and a button that copies a short link.

Pages are kept as Markdown, so **bold**, *italic*, headings, lists and quotes all work.

Predictable text is near the front. Gibberish is far in the back.

The first search downloads about 135 MB.
