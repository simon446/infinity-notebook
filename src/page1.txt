# Infinity Notebook

Every text that fits on one page (up to 2000 characters) already has a page in this notebook.

Write something here and press Enter, or tap "Find this page", to open the page it is on. Shift + Enter starts a new line.

Flip pages with the arrows, roll the dice for a random page, or type a page number at the top.

Predictable text is near the front. Gibberish is far in the back.

The first search downloads about 135 MB.
