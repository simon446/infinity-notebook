# Infinity Notebook

This notebook contains every piece of text that will ever exist (up to 2000 characters a page).

Go on, try it: change this text and press Enter, or tap "Find this page". Your words are already written down somewhere in here, and the notebook will open on that page.

Flip pages with the arrows, roll the dice for a random page, or tap the page number and type any number you like.

For a new line on a keyboard, press Shift + Enter.

The first search fetches the notebook's index, which is rather heavy (about 135 MB), so give it a moment. Tidy, predictable writing is filed near the front. Gibberish is kept far, far in the back.
